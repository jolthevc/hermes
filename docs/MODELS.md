## Rejected

- **TalkVerse:** Snap Non-Commercial License.
- **Wan2.2-S2V:** needs an 80 GB GPU.
- **daVinci-MagiHuman:** generates its own audio, so it can't use our voices.
- **HunyuanVideo-1.5:** community license appears to exclude the EU, UK and South Korea (unverified); we post globally.
